Just FYI helps you privately notify past intimate partners if you test positive for a sexually transmitted infection, and get notified if someone you've been with does the same.

## How It Works

1. **Record interactions** - When you meet someone, both open the app and your phones exchange IDs via Bluetooth
2. **Get notified** - If someone you've interacted with reports a positive test, you receive a private notification
3. **Notify others** - If you test positive, report it in the app to privately alert your past contacts

No awkward conversations. No public exposure. Just private, respectful notification.

## Why Just FYI?

### Completely Anonymous
You never create an account or provide personal information. Your anonymous ID cannot be traced back to you.

### No Awkward Conversations
Let the app handle the difficult task of notifying past partners. They'll know to get tested without knowing it came from you.

### Privacy-First
Your data is encrypted. We collect only what's needed to send notifications. No tracking, no ads, no data selling.

## Download Now

Available for Android and iOS. Free to use, with source code publicly available.
