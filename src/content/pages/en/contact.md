We would love to hear from you. Whether you have questions, feedback, or need support, here is how to reach us.

## Email Support

For general inquiries, support requests, or feedback:

**Email**: [support@justfyi.app](mailto:support@justfyi.app)

### What to Include

To help us assist you quickly, please include:

- A clear description of your question or issue
- Your device type (Android or iOS) and version
- App version (found in Settings)
- Any relevant screenshots (make sure they do not contain personal information)

### Response Times

We are a small team working on Just FYI as a community project. Please allow:

- **General inquiries**: 2-3 business days
- **Bug reports**: 1-2 business days for initial response
- **Security concerns**: Within 24 hours

We prioritize security-related communications and will respond to those as quickly as possible.

## Bug Reports and Feature Requests

For technical issues, bug reports, or feature suggestions, please use our GitHub Issues:

**GitHub Issues**: [github.com/JustFYI-App/JustFYI/issues](https://github.com/JustFYI-App/JustFYI/issues)

### Reporting a Bug

When reporting a bug, please include:

1. **Steps to reproduce** the issue
2. **Expected behavior** (what should happen)
3. **Actual behavior** (what actually happens)
4. **Device information** (model, OS version)
5. **App version**
6. **Screenshots or logs** if available

### Requesting a Feature

For feature requests:

1. Check existing issues to avoid duplicates
2. Describe the feature and its benefit
3. Explain your use case
4. Be open to discussion and feedback

## Security Vulnerabilities

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly:

**Do NOT** create a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities.

Instead, email us directly at **support@justfyi.app** with:

- Subject line: "Security Vulnerability Report"
- Detailed description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Potential impact assessment
- Your suggested fix (if any)

We take security seriously and will:

- Acknowledge your report within 24 hours
- Keep you informed of our progress
- Credit you in our release notes (if you wish)

## What We Cannot Help With

As a privacy-focused project, there are some things we cannot assist with:

- **Identifying users**: We do not have access to user identities
- **Retrieving lost data**: We store user data anonymously, but we cannot identify which account is yours
- **Medical advice**: Please consult healthcare professionals
- **Legal matters**: We cannot provide legal advice

## Frequently Asked Contact Questions

### I forgot my Anonymous ID. Can you help me recover it?

We do not have access to your Anonymous ID. It is stored only on your device. If you need a new one, you can generate a fresh Anonymous ID in the app settings.

### Can you tell me who sent me an exposure notification?

The notification system is designed for privacy. When you receive a notification, you will see the username of the person you interacted with (as recorded at the time of interaction), but only you and that person know about your interaction. We do not have access to this information.

### My app is not working. What should I do?

1. Make sure you have the latest version installed
2. Check that Bluetooth is enabled
3. Ensure the app has the necessary permissions
4. Try restarting the app or your device
5. If problems persist, email us with details

### How can I verify Just FYI is trustworthy?

Just FYI is source-available. You can:

- Review our code on GitHub
- Check security audits (if available)
- Read reviews from privacy advocates
- Ask questions in our community

Thank you for using Just FYI and for helping build a safer, more private community.
