Just FYI is designed to be simple, private, and effective. Here is how it works in four easy steps.

## Step 1: Download the App

Get Just FYI from your device's app store. The app is free, source-available, and designed with your privacy as the top priority.

**What happens during setup:**
- No account creation required
- No email or phone number needed
- No personal information collected
- Simply download and open the app

**Privacy at this step:** You remain completely anonymous. We do not know who you are.

## Step 2: Create Your Anonymous ID

When you first open Just FYI, a unique Anonymous ID is automatically generated for you.

**What is an Anonymous ID?**
- A random string of characters
- Generated securely when you first use the app
- Never linked to your real identity
- Can be regenerated at any time

**How it protects you:**
- No connection to your name, email, or phone
- Cannot be reverse-engineered to identify you
- Used only for anonymous interaction matching

**Privacy at this step:** Your Anonymous ID has no connection to your real identity.

## Step 3: Record Interactions

With Bluetooth enabled, Just FYI automatically and anonymously records interactions with other Just FYI users.

**How interactions are recorded:**
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) detects nearby devices
- Only devices with Just FYI are detected
- IDs and usernames are exchanged
- The interaction is recorded with a timestamp

**What is stored:**
- The ID of the other user
- Their username (as it was at the time of interaction)
- Approximate time of interaction

**Privacy at this step:** No location data is collected. Interaction data is encrypted and stored securely.

## Step 4: Receive Anonymous Notifications

If someone you interacted with reports a potential health exposure, you receive an anonymous notification.

**How notifications work:**
1. A user reports a potential exposure through the app
2. Their Anonymous ID is securely transmitted to our notification system
3. Users who interacted with that ID receive a notification
4. No identifying information is shared in any direction

**What you receive:**
- A notification that you may have been exposed
- Approximate date range of potential exposure
- Guidance on next steps

**What you do NOT receive:**
- Location or venue information
- Any details beyond what was exchanged during the interaction

**Privacy at this step:** Only people you directly interacted with can receive your notification, and they will see your username as it was recorded at the time of interaction.

## The Privacy Promise

At every step, Just FYI is designed to protect your privacy:

### No Personal Data Collection
We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifying information.

### Secure Data Storage
Interaction data is encrypted and stored securely. We use minimal data to enable the notification system while protecting your privacy.

### Automatic Data Deletion
Interaction history is automatically deleted after 180 days. You can delete your data at any time from your profile settings.

### Source-Available
Our code is publicly available for anyone to review. We have nothing to hide because we collect nothing to hide.

### No Tracking
No GPS, no location services, no behavioral tracking. Bluetooth is used only for proximity detection.

Download Just FYI today and join a community that values both health and privacy. Together, we can create safer spaces while respecting everyone's right to anonymity.
